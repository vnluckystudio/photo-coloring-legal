Privacy Policy — Photo Coloring

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Photo Coloring ("the App") is developed by Lucky Studio. It turns your photos into coloring pages that you color on your device. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

The short version

The App does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. It has no account or sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Your photos and artwork are processed and kept on your device and are never uploaded to a server.

On-device artificial intelligence

The "Sketch" style uses a small neural network that runs locally on your device (Apple Core ML). Your photo is not sent to a server or to any third party to create it. Credits and the licence of the model are shown in the App under Settings → Third-party notices.

Sharing

The App does not share data with third parties. When you choose Share or Save, your artwork goes only to the app or the photo library you pick.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from any user, it does not collect data from children either.

Your control

Delete any project inside the App, or delete the App to remove all of its data. You can withdraw camera or photo access at any time in iOS Settings → Photo Coloring.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL and the date above will change.
