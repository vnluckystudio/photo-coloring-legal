Common questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Photo Coloring.

Where are my photos and artwork stored?

On your device, in the App's private storage. Photos are never uploaded, except when you choose the optional AI style and agree: then that one photo is sent to draw the page and is not kept by us. Deleting a project, or the App, removes it from your device. See the Privacy Policy.

How do AI images work?

The AI style is for people 18 and over (the App asks you to confirm before first use). It is part of Premium, which includes 20 AI images; you can add 10 more at a time in the App. Each image uses 3 credits, up to 50 images a day. You are only charged when an image is delivered. See your balance in Settings → AI images. Full details: AI Terms of Use.

My coloring page looks different from my photo.

The App draws outlines from your photo. Bright, sharp, well-lit photos work best; try the Detailed or Sketch styles for more detail.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.
