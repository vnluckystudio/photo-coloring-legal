Coloring Pages by Lucky Support

For help with Coloring Pages by Lucky (shown as Photo Coloring in the app), contact Lucky Studio at vn.lucky.studio@gmail.com.

Restore full access

Open a locked page and choose Restore purchases. Sign in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Full access is a one-time purchase and does not renew.

Credits and rewarded ads

One credit unlocks one page on this device. If a rewarded ad is available, finish watching it to receive one credit. Ads may be unavailable depending on region, consent choices, network connection, or ad availability.

Projects and photos

Your coloring projects are stored on your device. To remove them, delete them in the app or remove the app and its local data. When you export finished artwork, iOS controls access to your photo library.

Collections

An internet connection is needed to download collection artwork. Pages that have been downloaded are cached on your device for offline coloring.
